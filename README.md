SB Hacks Project

Our inspiration for Maskerade came from the idea that connecting with people should be fun, safe, and pressure-free—like attending a masquerade ball, but in the digital age. We saw how social media can feel overwhelming, with oversharing, judgment, and privacy concerns, and thought, What if we could give people a space to connect without the fear of being ‘seen’?

Maskerade is Omegle with privacy and smart matching for strangers.
